Why another birthday app

Birthday apps go quiet when it counts.

The reminder never shows up

Phones quietly stop apps in the background to save battery. A birthday app that stays silent on the day is worse than none, and it's the most common complaint about apps like this.

Ads and paywalls in something simple

Video ads, nagging upgrades, and a "premium" that still holds things back. Remembering a birthday shouldn't come with a sales pitch.

How it works

Three steps, then forget about forgetting.

  1. Add your people

    Type them in, or bring them in from your contacts in one go.

  2. Take the 1-minute check-up

    BdayBoom checks notifications, alarms, Autostart and battery saver, then sends you a test.

  3. Get reminded on the day

    On the minute. Today's reminder stays put until you tap Wished.

What's different

Built around the reminder reaching you.

  • A check-up that proves itSpots what would stop a reminder and opens the exact setting to fix it. Then sends a real test, even with the app closed.
  • Today's reminder stays until you tap WishedClearing notifications by habit won't lose a birthday.
  • Contacts that stay in syncBrings in birthdays, anniversaries and dates without a year, and picks up dates you change later.
  • Dates that never shiftA birthday is a date, not a time, so it never slips a day when clocks or time zones change.
  • Photos and gift notesEvery person gets a face and a place for what they mentioned wanting.
  • A calendar for the whole monthSee who's coming up, and add someone on any day.
  • Widgets on your home screenThe next few birthdays, or one big countdown.
  • Backup to a fileMoving to a new phone? Save one file and restore it there.
  • Light and darkWarm by day, candle-lit by night.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, no ads, no account.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. BdayBoom has no server. Backups are files you save yourself.

I have a Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO. Will reminders work?

Yes, once Autostart is on. The check-up takes you to the right setting in one tap.

Can I import birthdays from my contacts?

Yes, and running it again picks up dates you changed in Contacts. There's no import from other birthday apps yet.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's on GitHub for now. BdayBoom updates itself from there and tells you when a new version is out.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the GNU GPL v3.0. The code is on GitHub; anyone can read it, build it, and suggest changes.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8.0 or newer.
