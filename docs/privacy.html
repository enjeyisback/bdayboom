Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Short version: BdayBoom doesn't collect your data. Everything stays on your phone.

What BdayBoom stores

The people you add (names, dates, photos, notes and groups) and your settings. They're stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. There is no account and no BdayBoom server.

Contacts

If you choose to import, BdayBoom reads the names, birthdays and anniversaries in your phone's contacts, on your phone, to add them to your list. It never uploads them, and it doesn't read anything else.

Photos

A photo you pick for a person is copied into the app's private storage, made smaller, and never leaves your phone.

Backups

A backup is a file that you save, wherever you choose. BdayBoom doesn't send it anywhere.

Network

The only time BdayBoom uses the internet is to check GitHub for a newer version and, if you tap Update, to download it. That request carries no information about you or your list. Sharing a wish uses the app you pick, such as WhatsApp, under that app's own privacy policy.

Ads, analytics and tracking

None.

Deleting your data

Remove anyone from your list at any time, or uninstall BdayBoom to delete everything it stored.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/enjeyisback/bdayboom/issues.
